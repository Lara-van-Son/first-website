The story of Jip

Tales of a wild, feverishly birght girl, who turned dark but found her spark.

I want to tell you a story. The story of a radiantly happy, somewhat extraordinary girl. The story of Jip. Jip had thick, reddish hair and cute freckles on her nose. Everyone loved Jip, because she was completely herself and inspired others to be themselves too. And if you didn’t like Jip? Well, she would do everything in her power to make sure you did—that was just the kind of person she was. Yet, everyone agreed on one thing: Jip radiated joy. Full of energy, Jip spent her days running through the woods and the village—preferably barefoot. You would most often see her with flushed cheeks and smudges on her clothes. To Jip, the world was full of warmth and wonder, and her days were filled with little moments of happiness that she chased after with a racing heart and a joyful shout.
